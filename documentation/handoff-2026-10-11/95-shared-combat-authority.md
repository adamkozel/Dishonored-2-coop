# Shared combat authority on missions

Stage 4, card #14. Candidates 1214n–1214x; harnesses 1270 (multi-enemy fight), 1271 (killing an unconscious guard), 1272 (host-world damage on a bound guard), 1273/1274 (host grenade on a bound guard).

The roadmap exit criteria for stage 4 are: enemies perceive and fight both players; one authoritative damage and outcome path; and synchronized health, deaths, spawns, bodies and physics. This chapter covers sustained multi-enemy fights on a real mission map and the two gaps they exposed in the shared outcome path. The NPC stream and the per-NPC combat channels are described in [chapter 86](86-mission-npc-authority.md).

## Multi-enemy fight harness (1270)

`Test-MissionMultiCombat-v1270*.ps1` is generated by `make_mission_multicombat_v1270*.py` from the Edge of the World product harness (1235f). It works like this:
- both peers load Edge of the World under the product controller;
- both players are placed by a guard group: every unique guard within 16 m and 3 m height of the `-Guard` anchor;
- for `-FightMs`, each player re-aims at its nearest living guard every 2.5 s and attacks every 0.7 s;
- every ~0.25 s it samples both peers' NPC-combat session (result, commits, deaths, retirements), NPC-stream entries, player health and each guard's health;
- at the end it compares every guard's state (dead, unconscious, alive) between the two worlds.

**Pass (runtime):** every guard ends in the same state in both worlds and at least one is defeated. A player death makes the fight inconclusive (1270c+).

## Unshared deaths: the Dead reconciliation (1214n)

**Finding (confirmed runtime, takes 1270c and 1270e):** guard 02 died on the host without a combat outcome:
- its combat channel was retired with `bound_actor_retired_without_combat_outcome`;
- the host never committed the death;
- the host rejected every hit request the client sent for it (`receive_binding`);
- the client's replica stayed alive.

Before 1214n, a host death that bypassed the combat capture was never shared.

**Fix (1214n):** a host → client `Dead` control message on the NPC stream.
- The host records when each streamed NPC becomes a corpse.
- 1.5 s later, if that NPC's combat channel holds neither a committed nor a pending death, the host sends `Dead`: up to five sends, 500 ms apart.
- The client kills its replica silently with the native `kill_no_reaction_damage` declaration.
  - The attacker is the world (`0x3fff0010`), never the client player.
  - The kill runs through the same native entry and lethal-subtraction ownership release as a shared death.
  - The stream keeps presenting the host's corpse.
- `Dead` for a replica that is already dead is counted and ignored.

`npcDeadReconcileDue` (in `npc_authority_protocol.h`) is the pure decision and is unit-tested. The stream JSON records each channel's `corpse`, `deathShare` (`combat`, `reconcile_sent` or `client_dead_from_host`) and `deadSends`, plus the counters `deadReconciled`, `deadSent`, `combatSharedDeaths`, `clientDeadApplied`, `clientDeadAlready` and `clientDeadRefused`.

**Result (take 1270e, confirmed runtime):** four guards, all dead in both worlds.
- **Host:** three deaths shared by combat commits, one (guard 02) reconciled.
- **Client:** applied the reconciliation (`client_dead_from_host`). The native declaration read amount 0 and flags `0xc` (kill), so the lethal subtraction removed the full health.

## Why guard 02 died unshared: host-world damage (1214o)

**Finding (confirmed runtime, take 1270e):** at 12.19 s the host retired guard 02's combat channel with `hit_damage_parameters`. The refused capture was `components/characters/animals/bloodfly/damage_periodic.damage`: bloodflies near the checkpoint stung the guard.

The capture had suppressed that hit and retired the channel. Then, during the 2 s re-bind block:
- the host's next three sword hits (30 → 20 → 5 → 0 between 12.2 and 13.6 s) applied natively and uncaptured;
- the client's hit requests were rejected;
- the death was never committed.

Such host-only health losses also blocked re-binding for good: the re-bind handshake compares both peers' health and retired the channel again on any difference.

**Fix (1214o):**
- **Host-world damage passes the host capture natively.** Host-world damage is damage where neither the attacker nor the inflictor is the local player, a player-owned entity or a world sentinel (`npcCombatHostWorldSource`). Corpse contacts and map triggers keep their exact replay.
- **HostWorld commit.** On the host, each bound channel tracks the health it last shared. When the native health drops below it without a commit, the host sends an ordered `HostWorld` commit of its observed loss.
- **Client follow.** The client removes exactly that loss with the native per-component health subtraction (health vtable `+0xf8`, where every native damage ends). Such a commit is never lethal and never raises health; deaths stay the combat outcome's or `Dead`'s.
- **Hello follow.** When the host's health in a re-bind Hello is lower than the client's, the client follows it the same way (the host waits for the client's corrected Hello) instead of retiring the NPC for good. A client that is lower than the host still retires (a replica never gains health).

The combat JSON records each channel's retirement reason and shared health, plus `hostWorldPassed`, `hostWorldCommitted`, `hostWorldApplied`, `hostWorldGains`, `helloFollowed`, `helloWaited` and `followSubtractions`.

**Result (take 1272b, 1214r, confirmed runtime):** `coop_npc_combat hostworld_test` is a lab-only fixture. On the host, it sends `bloodfly/damage_periodic.damage` (amount 5), with another streamed NPC as attacker and inflictor, through the installed damage observer at the nearest bound, conscious guard, exactly as a creature's hit enters it.
- Three hits on the Dunwall tutorial guard: host 40 → 35 → 30 → 25, and the client followed to exactly the same values.
- Counters: `hostWorldPassed` 3, `hostWorldCommitted` 3, `hostWorldApplied` 3 and `followSubtractions` 3; the combat session stayed `active_native_melee`.
- The natural case (bloodflies at the Edge of the World checkpoint) did not recur in 1270f/1270g.

## Killing an unconscious guard (1214p–1214v, harness 1271)

`Test-KillUnconscious-v1271.ps1` (generated by `make_kill_unconscious_v1271.py` from 1248l):
- the source chokes the Dunwall upper-floor tutorial guard 2 under the product controller, and both worlds must agree it is unconscious;
- the source then kills the body with sword attacks, looking down in place and then sweeping the yaw (the ragdoll lies at the source's feet, not at the guard's recorded root);
- **pass:** the guard ends dead in both worlds, and its bodies end less than 2 m apart.

**Findings (confirmed runtime):**
- **1271c:** an unconscious replica's AI is not in the paused state. The `Dead` kill's ownership check required the paused AI, so the lethal subtraction was blocked.
  - **Fix (1214p):** the reconciliation identity requires the exact incarnation, alive, in any AI state. The native kill may exceed the remaining health, and an unclean AI release no longer blocks the kill once the corpse is recorded.
- **1271e:** no combat channel bound an unconscious NPC, so the client's hit was never a request.
  - **Fix (1214q):** unconscious NPCs keep their combat channel; the client binds the replica by exact incarnation and life. Host hits on an unconscious NPC stay native (`hostUnconsciousPassed`): the loss is shared as `HostWorld` and the death as `Dead`.
- **1271h, client kill:** the client's hit became a request. The host's replay check failed (`native_replay_failed`), although the native damage had already killed the host's guard; `Dead` shared the death back.
  - **Cause (1214s diagnostics, take 1271i):** the result check failed on `health_maximum`. A native kill of an unconscious NPC changes its health maximum.
  - **Fix (1214t):** a terminal result may change the maximum. The commit keeps the pre-hit maximum, the dead proof checks the post-death one, and the change is counted as `maximumChanged`.
- **1271k, client kill:** the host now committed the death, but the client's own replay failed: releasing the unconscious replica's AI lease is not clean.
  - **Fix (1214u):** an unclean release no longer blocks the death once the corpse is recorded.
- **1271l, client kill:** both worlds recorded the death, but the bodies ended 8.84 m apart. The client had choked this guard, and its replica stays locally paired while unconscious (1214a) so that its native unconscious body is kept. Nothing ended that pairing on death, so the host's corpse poses arrived but were never applied: the root count stayed at 308 while poses rose from 543 to 581.
  - **Fix (1214v):** a corpse ends the pairing, so the host's corpse stream drives the body (1271m: 0.0 m apart).

## Host grenades and powers (1214w–1214x, harnesses 1273/1274)

**Finding (confirmed static):** the combat replay reproduces only these declarations exactly: player sword, kick, fatality, assassination, corpse contact and the three ranged profiles (gun spread, regular bullet, bolt). Any other player damage on a bound NPC, for example `explosion_grenade`, Windblast (`windblast_npc`), `springrazor` or the bolt variants, was suppressed by the capture, and the NPC's channel was retired (`hit_damage_parameters`). A host grenade therefore did not hurt a bound guard.

**Fix (1214w):** on the host, such damage applies natively (`npcCombatHostNativeDeclaration`, counted as `hostNativePassed` with the last declaration). HostWorld commits share a loss and `Dead` shares a death. World sentinels (corpse contact, map triggers) stay captured.

**Tests:**
- **1273a:** fail (harness). The lab item fixture refuses to grant a grenade under the product controller (`unsupported_scope_or_item_owned`): it needs the isolated movement fixture.
- **1274a (1214x), pass (confirmed runtime):** the lab-only `coop_npc_combat hostnative_test` sends the native `explosion_grenade` (amount 50; attacker and inflictor the host player) through the installed damage observer at the bound Dunwall tutorial guard.
  - The hit passed the capture natively (`hostNativePassed` 1) and killed the guard (40 → 0).
  - `Dead` shared the death: the client's replica also went 40 → 0 (`client_dead_from_host`).
- A real thrown grenade or power on a mission map is still to be recorded.

## Open

- A client's grenade or power on a host NPC is still suppressed and retires the channel; it needs a request the host applies natively.
- Host grenades and powers are verified through the lab fixture only (1274a), not yet with a real throw or cast on a mission.
- The Hello follow (host lower at re-bind) is unit-tested but not yet observed at runtime.
- Spawns, bodies and physics beyond the corpse stream are not covered by this chapter.
