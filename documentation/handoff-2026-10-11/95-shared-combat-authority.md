# Shared combat authority on missions

Stage 4, card #14. Candidates 1214n–1214ag; harnesses 1270 (multi-enemy fight), 1271 (killing an unconscious guard), 1272 (host-world damage on a bound guard), 1273/1274 (host grenade on a bound guard), 1275 (client grenade on a host guard) 1276 (real sleep darts and chokes on Edge of the World), 1277 (carrying a choked body), 1278 (the Hello follow) and 1279 (spawn census).

The roadmap exit criteria for stage 4 are: enemies perceive and fight both players; one authoritative damage and outcome path; and synchronized health, deaths, spawns, bodies and physics. This chapter covers sustained multi-enemy fights on a real mission map and the two gaps they exposed in the shared outcome path. The NPC stream and the per-NPC combat channels are described in [chapter 86](86-mission-npc-authority.md).

## Multi-enemy fight harness (1270)

`Test-MissionMultiCombat-v1270*.ps1` is generated by `make_mission_multicombat_v1270*.py` from the Edge of the World product harness (1235f). It works like this:
- both peers load Edge of the World under the product controller;
- both players are placed by a guard group: every unique guard within 16 m and 3 m height of the `-Guard` anchor;
- for `-FightMs`, each player re-aims at its nearest living guard every 2.5 s and attacks every 0.7 s;
- every ~0.25 s it samples both peers' NPC-combat session (result, commits, deaths, retirements), NPC-stream entries, player health and each guard's health;
- at the end it compares every guard's state (dead, unconscious, alive) between the two worlds.

**Pass (runtime):** every guard ends in the same state in both worlds and at least one is defeated. A player death makes the fight inconclusive (1270c+).

## Unshared deaths: the Dead reconciliation (1214n)

**Finding (confirmed runtime, takes 1270c and 1270e):** guard 02 died on the host without a combat outcome:
- its combat channel was retired with `bound_actor_retired_without_combat_outcome`;
- the host never committed the death;
- the host rejected every hit request the client sent for it (`receive_binding`);
- the client's replica stayed alive.

Before 1214n, a host death that bypassed the combat capture was never shared.

**Fix (1214n):** a host → client `Dead` control message on the NPC stream.
- The host records when each streamed NPC becomes a corpse.
- 1.5 s later, if that NPC's combat channel holds neither a committed nor a pending death, the host sends `Dead`: up to five sends, 500 ms apart.
- The client kills its replica silently with the native `kill_no_reaction_damage` declaration.
  - The attacker is the world (`0x3fff0010`), never the client player.
  - The kill runs through the same native entry and lethal-subtraction ownership release as a shared death.
  - The stream keeps presenting the host's corpse.
- `Dead` for a replica that is already dead is counted and ignored.

`npcDeadReconcileDue` (in `npc_authority_protocol.h`) is the pure decision and is unit-tested. The stream JSON records each channel's `corpse`, `deathShare` (`combat`, `reconcile_sent` or `client_dead_from_host`) and `deadSends`, plus the counters `deadReconciled`, `deadSent`, `combatSharedDeaths`, `clientDeadApplied`, `clientDeadAlready` and `clientDeadRefused`.

**Result (take 1270e, confirmed runtime):** four guards, all dead in both worlds.
- **Host:** three deaths shared by combat commits, one (guard 02) reconciled.
- **Client:** applied the reconciliation (`client_dead_from_host`). The native declaration read amount 0 and flags `0xc` (kill), so the lethal subtraction removed the full health.

## Why guard 02 died unshared: host-world damage (1214o)

**Finding (confirmed runtime, take 1270e):** at 12.19 s the host retired guard 02's combat channel with `hit_damage_parameters`. The refused capture was `components/characters/animals/bloodfly/damage_periodic.damage`: bloodflies near the checkpoint stung the guard.

The capture had suppressed that hit and retired the channel. Then, during the 2 s re-bind block:
- the host's next three sword hits (30 → 20 → 5 → 0 between 12.2 and 13.6 s) applied natively and uncaptured;
- the client's hit requests were rejected;
- the death was never committed.

Such host-only health losses also blocked re-binding for good: the re-bind handshake compares both peers' health and retired the channel again on any difference.

**Fix (1214o):**
- **Host-world damage passes the host capture natively.** Host-world damage is damage where neither the attacker nor the inflictor is the local player, a player-owned entity or a world sentinel (`npcCombatHostWorldSource`). Corpse contacts and map triggers keep their exact replay.
- **HostWorld commit.** On the host, each bound channel tracks the health it last shared. When the native health drops below it without a commit, the host sends an ordered `HostWorld` commit of its observed loss.
- **Client follow.** The client removes exactly that loss with the native per-component health subtraction (health vtable `+0xf8`, where every native damage ends). Such a commit is never lethal and never raises health; deaths stay the combat outcome's or `Dead`'s.
- **Hello follow.** When the host's health in a re-bind Hello is lower than the client's, the client follows it the same way (the host waits for the client's corrected Hello) instead of retiring the NPC for good. A client that is lower than the host still retires (a replica never gains health).

The combat JSON records each channel's retirement reason and shared health, plus `hostWorldPassed`, `hostWorldCommitted`, `hostWorldApplied`, `hostWorldGains`, `helloFollowed`, `helloWaited` and `followSubtractions`.

**Result (take 1272b, 1214r, confirmed runtime):** `coop_npc_combat hostworld_test` is a lab-only fixture. On the host, it sends `bloodfly/damage_periodic.damage` (amount 5), with another streamed NPC as attacker and inflictor, through the installed damage observer at the nearest bound, conscious guard, exactly as a creature's hit enters it.
- Three hits on the Dunwall tutorial guard: host 40 → 35 → 30 → 25, and the client followed to exactly the same values.
- Counters: `hostWorldPassed` 3, `hostWorldCommitted` 3, `hostWorldApplied` 3 and `followSubtractions` 3; the combat session stayed `active_native_melee`.
- The natural case (bloodflies at the Edge of the World checkpoint) did not recur in 1270f/1270g.

## Killing an unconscious guard (1214p–1214v, harness 1271)

`Test-KillUnconscious-v1271.ps1` (generated by `make_kill_unconscious_v1271.py` from 1248l):
- the source chokes the Dunwall upper-floor tutorial guard 2 under the product controller, and both worlds must agree it is unconscious;
- the source then kills the body with sword attacks, looking down in place and then sweeping the yaw (the ragdoll lies at the source's feet, not at the guard's recorded root);
- **pass:** the guard ends dead in both worlds, and its bodies end less than 2 m apart.

|---|---|---|---|
| 1271a | 1214o | host | Did not run: harness pinned to 1214n |
| 1271b | 1214o | host | Fail (harness): ten swings at the guard's recorded root hit an empty aisle |

| 1271d | 1214p | host | Pass: dead in both worlds, 0 m apart; `Dead` applied (`client_dead_from_host`) |

| 1271f/g | 1214q | both | Harness errors: one-string `-Sources`; pitch 72 over the helper's 70 limit |
| 1271h | 1214q | both | Pass in both roles; the client kill only through `Dead` after a failed host replay |
| 1271i | 1214s | client | Pass (diagnostics): the host replay failed on `health_maximum` |
| 1271j | 1214t | both | Interrupted: the PC was powered off 30 s into the take (12:53); no outcome |
| 1271k | 1214t | both | Client case: dead in both worlds, committed by the host; the client's own replay failed (`native_replay_failed`). Host case: pitch helper failure (harness) |
| 1271l | 1214u | both | Client case: committed and applied in both worlds, but the bodies ended 8.84 m apart. Host case: 15 swings missed |
| 1271m | 1214v | both | **Pass in both roles, bodies 0.0 m apart.** Client kill shared by the combat commit, host kill by `Dead` |

**Findings (confirmed runtime):**
- **1271c:** an unconscious replica's AI is not in the paused state. The `Dead` kill's ownership check required the paused AI, so the lethal subtraction was blocked.
  - **Fix (1214p):** the reconciliation identity requires the exact incarnation, alive, in any AI state. The native kill may exceed the remaining health, and an unclean AI release no longer blocks the kill once the corpse is recorded.
- **1271e:** no combat channel bound an unconscious NPC, so the client's hit was never a request.
  - **Fix (1214q):** unconscious NPCs keep their combat channel; the client binds the replica by exact incarnation and life. Host hits on an unconscious NPC stay native (`hostUnconsciousPassed`): the loss is shared as `HostWorld` and the death as `Dead`.
- **1271h, client kill:** the client's hit became a request. The host's replay check failed (`native_replay_failed`), although the native damage had already killed the host's guard; `Dead` shared the death back.
  - **Cause (1214s diagnostics, take 1271i):** the result check failed on `health_maximum`. A native kill of an unconscious NPC changes its health maximum.
  - **Fix (1214t):** a terminal result may change the maximum. The commit keeps the pre-hit maximum, the dead proof checks the post-death one, and the change is counted as `maximumChanged`.
- **1271k, client kill:** the host now committed the death, but the client's own replay failed: releasing the unconscious replica's AI lease is not clean.
  - **Fix (1214u):** an unclean release no longer blocks the death once the corpse is recorded.
- **1271l, client kill:** both worlds recorded the death, but the bodies ended 8.84 m apart. The client had choked this guard, and its replica stays locally paired while unconscious (1214a) so that its native unconscious body is kept. Nothing ended that pairing on death, so the host's corpse poses arrived but were never applied: the root count stayed at 308 while poses rose from 543 to 581.
  - **Fix (1214v):** a corpse ends the pairing, so the host's corpse stream drives the body (1271m: 0.0 m apart).

## Host grenades and powers (1214w–1214x, harnesses 1273/1274)

**Finding (confirmed static):** the combat replay reproduces only these declarations exactly: player sword, kick, fatality, assassination, corpse contact and the three ranged profiles (gun spread, regular bullet, bolt). Any other player damage on a bound NPC, for example `explosion_grenade`, Windblast (`windblast_npc`), `springrazor` or the bolt variants, was suppressed by the capture, and the NPC's channel was retired (`hit_damage_parameters`). A host grenade therefore did not hurt a bound guard.

**Fix (1214w):** on the host, such damage applies natively (`npcCombatHostNativeDeclaration`, counted as `hostNativePassed` with the last declaration). HostWorld commits share a loss and `Dead` shares a death. World sentinels (corpse contact, map triggers) stay captured.

**Tests:**
- **1273a:** fail (harness). The lab item fixture refuses to grant a grenade under the product controller (`unsupported_scope_or_item_owned`): it needs the isolated movement fixture.
- **1274a (1214x), pass (confirmed runtime):** the lab-only `coop_npc_combat hostnative_test` sends the native `explosion_grenade` (amount 50; attacker and inflictor the host player) through the installed damage observer at the bound Dunwall tutorial guard.
  - The hit passed the capture natively (`hostNativePassed` 1) and killed the guard (40 → 0).
  - `Dead` shared the death: the client's replica also went 40 → 0 (`client_dead_from_host`).
- A real thrown grenade or power on a mission map is still to be recorded.

## Client grenades and powers: PeerNative (1214y–1214z, harness 1275)

**Finding (confirmed static):** on the client, the same non-replayable player damage was suppressed and retired the NPC, so a client grenade, power, tranquilizer bolt or bolt variant never hurt a host NPC.

**Fix (1214z):**
- **Client:** such a hit becomes a `PeerNative` request carrying only its known declaration key and native amount (`validNpcPeerNative`: a known declaration that is not replayable, amount 0–1000, no geometry). The hit is suppressed locally.
- **Host:** applies the request natively with the partner avatar as attacker and inflictor (`npcCombatApplyPeerNative`). HostWorld commits share a loss and `Dead` shares a death.
- `PeerNative` is never a commit. It waits while sword requests are pending (`peerNativeBusy`).
- Counters: `peerNativeRequests` on the client, `peerNativeApplied` on the host.
- 1214y failed its own new unit test (the test lacked the client-side binding) and was never staged; 1214z is the same code with the fixed test.

**Tests (lab fixture `hostnative_test` run on the client, native `explosion_grenade` amount 50):**
- 1275a: the take stopped before running (candidate 1214y missing).
- 1275b: the path worked but the harness failed. The host applied the request (40 → 0) and `Dead` killed the client's replica (40 → 0); the harness wrongly expected the client's own hit to land.
- **1275c: pass.** Host 40 → 0 and client 40 → 0; `peerNativeRequests` 1 and `peerNativeApplied` 1.
- **Regressions on 1214z:** 1274b (host grenade, 40 → 0 in both worlds) and 1272c (HostWorld 40 → 35 → 30 → 25 in both worlds) pass.

## Players apart: relevance fairness and the NPC life roster (1214ab–1214ad, harness 1276)

`Test-MissionRanged-v1276.ps1` (generated by `make_mission_ranged_v1276.py` from 1270d) works like this:
- the pair loads Edge of the World under the product controller;
- one source acts on a checkpoint guard while the other player stays at the docks spawn, about 140 m away;
- the action is either a sleep dart (quick slot D-pad Up, LT) or a rear choke;
- both worlds are polled until they agree, for up to 16 s.

**Pass (runtime):** the target leaves the alive state and ends in the same state in both worlds.

**Findings (confirmed runtime):**
- **1276a:** the host's dart knocked the guard out on the host only, and Emily's dart struck a pillar before the guards killed her (Game Over). Takes 1276b–1276d then missed: the press fell into the placement autosave, the patrolling guard had moved, and shots at its back from 3 m missed.
- **1276e:** a frontal dart knocked the guard out on the host within 1.8 s; the client stayed awake for 12 s. The guard had no NPC-stream entry in either world.
- **1276f/1276g:** relevance binds the 24 nearest NPCs within 55 m of either player. NPCs around the client's spawn held every slot.
  - 1214ab: let the farthest bound NPC give way. It could not evict them, because they were near a player too.
  - 1214ac: fair ranking per player, so each player keeps its own nearest NPCs.
  - The guard still stayed unbound.
- **1276h (diagnostics):** the guard was in neither world's eligible catalog. On the host it had already been alerted by Corvo standing in front of it. On the client, NPCs this far from Emily were mostly ineligible (88 ineligible, 3 eligible), likely because their AI is not loaded (hypothesis). NPCs near only one player therefore cannot be streamed at all.

**Fix (1214ad): the NPC life roster.**
- The off-thread catalog worker records the life state (alive, unconscious, dead) of every unique, reciprocal NPC.
- Both roles read it every 2 s; the client now also requests catalogs periodically, and that snapshot serves host Hellos too.
- When an **unbound** NPC reaches a more severe state, a `Life` control (spawner, definition, state) goes to the peer.
- The peer applies the state once, silently, to its own unbound NPC of the same spawner and definition: a knockout with `sleep_no_reaction`, or a kill with `kill_no_reaction`, the world as attacker.
- Bound NPCs stay with the stream and the combat channels. States from the peer are never echoed back.
- Counters: `lifeSent`, `lifeReceived`, `lifeApplied` and `lifeRefused` in the stream relevance block.

**Results:**
- **1276i (host dart), pass:** the host knocked the guard out at 1.2 s, and the client followed at 3.4 s (host sent 1, client applied 1). The 24 states the client reported at session start, such as scripted corpses, matched the host's: it applied none.
- **1276j/1276k (client dart):** failed and inconclusive. Emily was first placed from the host world's guard position; this unbound guard patrols separately in each world (fixed in 1276k: each source aims from its own world). Her darts then missed, and in 1276k the guards killed her.
- **1276l:** the guards had spotted Emily during the bind wait, so the choke was blocked.
- **1276m (client choke), pass:** Emily choked the guard in her own world (unconscious at 3.6 s), and the host followed at 7.7 s (host applied 1).

**Limits:**
- Up to about 4 s of lag (two 2 s scan cycles).
- A state is applied once: an NPC that wakes up in one world stays unconscious in the other.
- An NPC killed outside relevance dies silently in the other world, with no reaction.

**Regressions on 1214ad:**
- 1270h (three-guard fight, both players): every guard dead in both worlds.
- 1271n: the host-kill case passed; the client case failed because its choke never started (the guard moved or noticed Emily).
- 1271o (client case re-run): pass, the kill committed by the host, bodies 0.0 m apart.

## Spawn census and a relevance crash (1214ag, harness 1279)

`Test-MissionSpawnCensus-v1279.ps1` (generated by `make_mission_spawn_census_v1279.py` from 1270d) works like this:
- both worlds' unique NPCs are recorded;
- the host visits the mission's `script_trigger*` volumes one by one (teleport in, then back to its spot; trigger mirroring replays each enter and exit for Emily);
- both lists are recorded again.

**Pass:** the NPCs added and removed are identical in both worlds.

**1279a: host crash (confirmed runtime and static).** The host game exited during the trigger walk.
- `loader.log` recorded an access violation in `dinput8.dll` at offset 0xA4583.
- A symbol build with the lab cache's exact flags matched the deployed `.text` bytes. dbghelp resolved the crash to `addRelevantNpcs`, `npc_authority_stream.h:280`.
- Cause: the 1214ac relevance code kept raw pointers into `npcChannels`, and `bindNpcChannel` appends to that vector. A reallocation left the pointers dangling, which the trigger walk exposed by binding many NPCs in one scan.
- **Fix (1214ag):** bound NPCs are tracked by arm.

**1279b (1214ag), pass:**
- 40 trigger volumes visited (the first 40 by name), with no crash.
- No NPC spawned. One civilian (`script_spawner_streets_wol_civilian_01`) despawned, in both worlds.
- Both worlds' unique NPC sets were identical before and after.
- This is a first data point only: no scripted spawn has been observed yet.

**1279c/1279d (1214ag), all 102 trigger volumes (`-Skip` added for the remainder):**
- 1279c visited 80 volumes before a player died (Game Over), so it is inconclusive. Up to then nothing spawned, and both worlds' unique NPC sets stayed identical.
- 1279d visited the remaining 22 volumes and passed, with no spawn and no despawn.
- Across all of Edge of the World's script triggers, no NPC spawned. Its NPCs appear to load with the map in both worlds, and the trigger-driven changes seen (one despawn) occur in both (hypothesis for missions not yet walked).

  - sends a `Carry` control every 200 ms;
  - sends the body's pose as `PairedPose` every 50 ms;
  - does not apply host poses to that body.
- **Host.** While a `Carry` is fresh, the host accepts those poses for the unconscious or dead NPC and holds its physics animated, so its body follows the client's.

## The NPC attack relay under dynamic relevance (1214ah)

The NPC attack relay carries a guard's sword hit at the partner's clone into the partner's own world, where it lands, is blocked or is parried natively.

**Finding (confirmed runtime, 1258i/1258j on 1214ag):** Emily's health stayed 70 in every 1270 fight, and she could never counter. During the 1258j case the relay was off on both peers: the host had stopped with `no_ready_actors` and the client with `negotiation_timeout`.

**Cause (confirmed static):** the relay bound only the NPCs that were ready when it started. It stopped for good on:
- an empty start (`no_ready_actors`);
- any retired NPC (`actor_retired`);
- any binding that missed the 5 s negotiation (`negotiation_timeout`);
- a peer re-arm with a new nonce (`peer_replaced`).

Nothing re-armed it outside checkpoint recovery, while the combat channels already join and retire with relevance (1213v).

**Fix (1214ah):**
- Every tick, the relay binds each ready, bound, living combat channel and retires bindings whose channel is gone. A retired NPC is not re-added for 3 s.
- A binding that does not negotiate in 5 s retires alone. Contacts from bindings that are not ready yet are dropped and counted.
- A peer re-arm restarts the local event sequence instead of stopping.
- In product mode, a relay that was requested but stopped re-arms after 3 s (outside checkpoint recovery).
- `npc-attack.json` gains `relevance` counters: wanted, rearms, added, retired, peer restarts, dropped contacts, last retirement reason.
