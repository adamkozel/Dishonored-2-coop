## Confirmed decompilation and remaining hypotheses

[819 transcript](../../work/natural-moveset/native-pickup-research-v785/ghidra-food-restorative-v819.json) traces `1406156e0` food handling, `14071a140` signed-health dispatch, `140719fe0` positive healing, `1407467d0` mana restoration and the native maximum/getter paths. Native food health is declaration+0x78 plus the inventory food attribute; mana is declaration+0x7c. The health amount in all27 bound Dunwall food objects is5; mana amount is0.

For the player, positive healing queues at health+0xf4 using `min(max(current, old target) + amount, native maximum)`. Actor+0xd1 bit8 suppresses this healing. Mana restoration queues at mana+0x2c and respects native maximum derived from declaration/attributes. The current/maximum getter also depends on the native mana-enabled global. Therefore reading only current health immediately after the reward can miss a legitimate queued heal.

**Correction to the initial819 uncertainty:** the overflow option at`140399620` is an unconditional false stub in this exact executable: `32 c0 c3` (`xor al,al; ret`). The [saved824 decompilation/disassembly](../../work/natural-moveset/native-pickup-research-v785/ghidra-food-overflow-v824.json) confirms it; candidate821 verifies these bytes before accepting food outcomes. Health-to-mana or mana-to-health overflow conversion is inactive here. This finding is saved in the persistent Ghidra project under bookmark category`D2Coop_FoodOutcome_824`.

[820 read-only state](../../work/natural-moveset/native-restorative-state-v820/) establishes actual native health56/max70, mana100/max100, food grant5 and stored food0 before deployment. The first822 source receipt records current health still56 while its target becomes61, directly confirming the queued-heal interpretation at runtime. Native mana-food rewards, attribute-modified food and suppression are covered by decompilation and unit cases, not actual consumable tests in this map. Do not present those branches as runtime certified.
