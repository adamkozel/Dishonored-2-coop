## Confirmed failure

The [v109 receiver-menu Mesmerize test](../../work/natural-moveset/mesmerize-links-receiver-pause-v109/result.json) failed on the v101 target-effect DLL. After the host opened and closed its native pause menu, the client exited around video 22 seconds. Checkpoint cleanup could not complete because that process had exited. All fourteen protected save/settings hashes still matched. The failed receipt is preserved; it must not be called a successful interruption test.

The [client log](../../work/natural-moveset/mesmerize-links-receiver-pause-v109/crash-snapshot/client/loader.log) records access violation `c0000005`, native RVA `584660`, read address `28`, caller `5a4cbe`. Its targeted diagnostic identifies `arkBTTask_Action_CombatGiveUp`, vtable RVA `1b8aa68`, task phase at `+b0` equal to 1, and context at `+b8` null. Actor handle `0c8311e6` (209916390) exactly matches the registered client-side Corvo presentation clone in the [captured status](../../work/natural-moveset/mesmerize-links-receiver-pause-v109/crash-snapshot/client/coop-status.json). This resolves the earlier uncertainty about whether this crash belonged to a world guard or our avatar, for this particular occurrence.

[Fresh exact-executable Ghidra decompilation](../../work/ghidra-combatgiveup-v111.json) shows `1405a4b00` calling `140584660(task->context)` in phase 1 without a null check. The saved project bookmark at `1405a4b00` was updated. This confirms the failing call and owner; it does not establish why the native task initially lacks its context.

The same snapshot shows movement follow disabled and the avatar AI reference released. `followProbe` calls `releaseAvatarAi` when either player leaves gameplay, including a native menu. A remote presentation clone must not become an autonomous NPC merely because its movement stream stopped. The [source sheet](../../work/natural-moveset/mesmerize-links-receiver-pause-v109/source-sheet.jpg) and [receiver sheet](../../work/natural-moveset/mesmerize-links-receiver-pause-v109/peer-sheet.jpg) were inspected: body/beam are visible before the menu, receiver cosmetics retire afterward, and the source beam persists until the exit. These are failed-run evidence, not visual acceptance.
