# Native Mesmerize investigation v70–v71

## Purpose and preservation

The next uncovered Emily power is Mesmerize. This investigation connects the exact native inventory, controller input, cast scenes and spawned effects before implementing remote body or world presentation. Corvo/Emily elixir movement results and the v68 DLL remain archived. This source-only experiment uses the mutable clone and full isolated checkpoint recovery. It never directly invokes native spawn, damage or AI APIs and makes no remote integration claim.
