# Movement completion coverage (#11, stage 3)

Updated 2026-10-18. This chapter covers how checklist items in [movement requirements](generated/movement-requirements.md) are closed: the coverage harnesses that drive every case under the product controller in both roles; what they found; the two receiver fixes they led to (candidates 1214cg and 1214ch); and the completion audits that record each item's decision in `review-catalog.json`.

## Coverage harnesses

All coverage harnesses share one base, `Record-Coverage-v1318.ps1`, generated by [make_coverage_v1318.py](../../work/natural-moveset/make_coverage_v1318.py) from the 1287 coverage harness. Each later harness is generated from it by its own `make_coverage_v13xx.py`.

**What every case does:**
1. Arms the product controller on both peers (`coop_product_auto on`) and waits until the partner presentation is live.
2. Places the source and the observer.
3. Films both windows (ffmpeg `gfxcapture`, 30 fps).
4. Drives the source with simulated controller input only. No keystroke reaches the desktop.
5. About every 125 ms, samples the observer's presentation status of the partner (`coop-status.json.movement.json`) together with the source's native player states.
6. Reloads the checkpoint, then checks the restored inventory and health.

The partner is always checked from its own presentation state on the observer: direction sectors, gait mixes, stance, lean, air, slide, traversal, combat, equipment and the presentation weight.

**Run:**

```
pwsh -File Record-Coverage-v13xx.ps1 -Take coverage-v13xxN -Candidate candidate-npc-combat-v1214ch [-Cases "a,b"]
```

Rules:
- Use a new take name every time.
- Under `pwsh -File`, a `-Cases` list arrives as one string. The harness splits it and refuses anything that is not a plain case name (take 1321b ran a single case named with the whole list).

## Lab geometry and native input facts

These are confirmed runtime. The probes are kept as evidence.

**Geometry** (probes `course-probe-v1321a`, `d`, `e`):
- From the lab spot (−35, −153), facing +x, a desk sits at x −29.2. It is the vault fixture.
- Behind the spot, at x −35.75, is the keyhole door.
- From (−34, −153) facing +y there is a straight 10 m lane at run and at sprint. From x −35, runs drift into an obstacle.
- The running course now starts at (−34, −155.4).

**Native input:**
- The native lean (player state 18) persists after Y is released. It ends on a Y tap, forward movement, a turn or the opposite side stick. Stepping back or crouching keeps it (probe `lean-exit-probe-v1322a`).
- A tap sent while a held input still holds the same button is no press. Lean exits are timed from the actual lean entry.
- Each `Send-D2Input` replaces the whole pad state. A jump during a run must keep the stick held, or the runner stops at landing (1321d).
- A sprint with A pressed near the low obstacle at about y −147 becomes a native vault (state 9).
- Native state 53 is the equipment scene (draw and holster).

**Re-placing the source mid-case** (`setviewpos`) is a teleport. The partner presentation fades through it by design: weight 0, then 1 over about 0.5 s. Weight samples within 1.2 s after a deliberate re-placement are therefore not counted as drops.

## Receiver fixes

**1214cg: the protocol-73 hold keeps the authored body.**
- For a packet or two, the source's action reader may not classify a native state combination (read stage 2). This happened at a holster end, a draw start, a holstered landing and a slide end.
- The source then sends the protocol-73 hold (state 24; see [chapter 82](82-water-exit-and-action-stream-hold.md)).
- Before 1214cg the receiver yielded the body to the actor's native pose (bank weight 0). At 15 fps, Corvo's holstered landing showed about two upright frames between the air pose and the landing crouch (take 1321a).
- 1214cg makes state 24 action-eligible. The body continues on the replicated physical state.
- Result: take 1320c shows zero low-weight samples where 1320b had 2–4 per role, and the receiver recorded no eligibility failure where it had 6.

**1214ch: a 150 ms grace before yielding.**
- At a classification boundary the root motion can be invalid for one packet (mask 2), and an action can still be unclassified. Cases seen: a keyhole entry (1319d) and a landing inside a sword action (1324a).
- The receiver used to hard-zero the weight on any ineligible packet and blend back over about 0.4 s.
- Now it holds the presented pose (clock frozen, weight kept) for at most 150 ms. A native shadow-action actor (mask 64) still takes over at once.
- The movement status counts `eligibility.graceFrames`.
- Result (take 1319e): the host keyhole case had 3 ineligible packets, all held (`graceFrames` 3), and no weight drop.

## Completion audits

An audit (`completion-audit-v13xx-<family>.json`) records the following for each item:
- the decision (complete or open) and the scope it covers;
- the coverage takes and cases it relies on;
- the reviewed observer videos and their verdicts;
- what remains, when the item stays open.

[apply_completion_audit.py](../../work/natural-moveset/apply_completion_audit.py) checks two things: that every cited case passed in its take's `result.json`, and that every cited video exists. If anything fails, it refuses the whole audit. Otherwise it records the decision on the item (`phase_status`, `complete`, `completion`) and regenerates the requirements document.

Visual review uses contact sheets of the observer videos, cropped to the partner and brightened when dark, at 3–15 fps around each action. The sheets are working files and are not committed.
