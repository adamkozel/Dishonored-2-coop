## Coverage harnesses

All coverage harnesses share one base, `Record-Coverage-v1318.ps1`, generated by [make_coverage_v1318.py](../../work/natural-moveset/make_coverage_v1318.py) from the 1287 coverage harness. Each later harness is generated from it, or from the 1321 traversal harness, by its own `make_coverage_v13xx.py`. A change to the base therefore means regenerating the whole family (1318 → 1319, 1320, 1321, 1330, 1332; 1321 → 1324, 1326, 1333).

**What every case does:**
1. Arms the product controller on both peers (`coop_product_auto on`) and waits until the partner presentation is live.
2. Places the source and the observer.
3. Films both windows (ffmpeg `gfxcapture`, 30 fps).
4. Drives the source with simulated controller input only. No keystroke reaches the desktop.
5. About every 125 ms, samples the observer's presentation status of the partner (`coop-status.json.movement.json`) together with the source's native player states.
6. Reloads the checkpoint, then checks the restored inventory and health. A failed restore is retried once.

Before its first case, every take restores the lab note baseline and only then reads the starting inventory. In take 1332b, a client left one note short by an earlier probe's bare checkpoint reload failed its first restore check against the wrong starting inventory.

The partner is always checked from its own presentation state on the observer: direction sectors, gait mixes, stance, lean, air, slide, traversal, combat, equipment, ranged, grenade, trap, Heart, use, power, swim, climb and the presentation weight.

Rules:
- Use a new take name every time.
- Under `pwsh -File`, a `-Cases` list arrives as one string. The harness splits it and refuses anything that is not a plain case name (take 1321b ran a single case named with the whole list).
- In a PowerShell plan table, a one-step plan must be written `@(,@(...))`: `@(@(...))` flattens into the step itself (take 1332a never pressed LT for seven powers).

## Receiver fixes

**1214cg: the protocol-73 hold keeps the authored body.**
- For a packet or two, the source's action reader may not classify a native state combination (read stage 2). This happened at a holster end, a draw start, a holstered landing and a slide end.
- The source then sends the protocol-73 hold (state 24; see [chapter 82](82-water-exit-and-action-stream-hold.md)).
- Before 1214cg the receiver yielded the body to the actor's native pose (bank weight 0). At 15 fps, Corvo's holstered landing showed about two upright frames between the air pose and the landing crouch (take 1321a).
- 1214cg makes state 24 action-eligible. The body continues on the replicated physical state.
- Result: take 1320c shows zero low-weight samples where 1320b had 2–4 per role.

**1214ch: a 150 ms grace before yielding.**
- At a classification boundary the root motion can be invalid for one packet (mask 2), and an action can still be unclassified. Cases seen: a keyhole entry (1319d) and a landing inside a sword action (1324a).
- The receiver now holds the presented pose (clock frozen, weight kept) for at most 150 ms instead of hard-zeroing the weight. A native shadow-action actor (mask 64) still takes over at once.
- The movement status counts `eligibility.graceFrames`.

**1214ci: a traversal settling allowance.** The body clock no longer resets at a vault exit: while the traversal weight is still above zero, the vertical bound of the low mantle applies.

**1214cj: the grounded vertical bound scales with the frame.** A grounded step is allowed `max(0.15 m, 6 m/s × dt)`, so a 4.8 m/s step down a ledge in a long frame keeps the body clock (unit test "a 4.8 m/s grounded step in a long frame keeps the body clock").

**1214cl: re-anchor on a fresh vertical refusal.** When the clock refuses a sample for its vertical bound (code 3) while the partner is eligible and was presented, it re-anchors on the received position instead of fading out (`clockRejection.reanchors`). Jump-attack landings no longer reset the body.

**1214cn: the damaging landing** (built, deployed after the 1328e chain).
- An ~11 m fall plays a different body scene in the same CatFall state: `cat_fall_strong`, 1066 ms, Corvo `Scene_2`, Emily `Scene_1`, phase 3. Confirmed runtime by the read-only midnight watch in mission-coverage-v1328e. The scene name also appears in the game's resource index (confirmed static).
- The landing reader accepted only the light scene (`cat_fall_light`, 1000 ms), so the partner never presented the hard landing (1328d, 1328e).
- 1214cn accepts both scenes, each only at its own observed duration, and the traversal presentation accepts variant 6 at 1000 or 1066 ms.

## Completion audits

An audit (`completion-audit-v13xx-<family>.json`) records the following for each item:
- the decision (complete or open) and the scope it covers;
- the coverage takes and cases it relies on;
- the reviewed observer videos and their verdicts;
- what remains, when the item stays open.

[apply_completion_audit.py](../../work/natural-moveset/apply_completion_audit.py) checks two things: that every cited case passed in its take's `result.json`, and that every cited video exists. If anything fails, it refuses the whole audit. Otherwise it records the decision on the item (`phase_status`, `complete`, `completion`) and regenerates the requirements document.
