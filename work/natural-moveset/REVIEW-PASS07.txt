Open review.html from this directory. The running local review server is also

482 paired Corvo/Emily authoring recordings and 7 actual D2 recordings are

HOW TO REVIEW
Use Requirements for one movement group, Clips for the entire recorded library,
or D2 for actual engine captures. Filter by movement family or search. Previous
and Next Clip walk through the current view. Play Next Automatically plays the
recorded clips in that view in sequence. Use quarter/half speed and frame steps
for contact checks. Character labels follow the camera orientation.

Choose Corvo, Emily or Both before writing feedback. Add Current Timestamp helps
identify the precise pose. Saved notes stay in this browser; Export Feedback
JSON preserves them outside the browser. Feedback keys include animation hashes
so a later revision does not silently reuse comments on different poses.

VALIDATION AND LIMITS
All 489 selected videos decode completely and match their saved animation bytes.
104 paired candidates have matching both-role fixed-frame D2 publication evidence
in the catalog. Additional gait tests cover input/network-driven travel. The new
18 role clips passed pose publication, cancellation, expiry and peer-pause checks.
These numerical checks do not establish visual quality or gameplay binding.

Authoring recordings are 30 fps. Animation data uses the source/declared timing.
Actual game captures preserve their measured intervals; capture rates are shown
in the viewer and are lower than the game's rendering rate. Do not judge in-game
motion smoothness from a 7-11 Hz capture as though it were a 30 fps recording.

KNOWN WORK STILL REQUIRED
- Floor lift gathering has actor/head-to-target overlap and imperfect weight transfer.
- Throw anticipation/follow-through and target settling need refinement.
- Aerial/sliding pairs have known impact/contact and recovery issues.
- Fine hand, blade, equipment and obstacle contact needs visual revision.
- Elixirs still use an NPC drinking donor; player-use behavior and correct held
  props are unverified. Generic donor names do not establish native action coverage.
- No-mana failure, arbitrary interruptions, power upgrades, native Shadow Walk
  morphology, extra enemy body sizes and several contextual variants are incomplete.
- Native physics, target ownership, equipment, action-state and campaign bindings
  need further implementation and testing. Grey targets/neutral fixtures are offline
  references. Ragdoll-time freezes and illustrative flights are visibly labelled.

CORVO CALF CORRECTION
The user identified a shared offline calf/boot deformation after the initial
recordings. The preview mistakenly drove calf helpers from the foot pivot.
The corrected preview anchors them to the shin, matching native D2 position.
See boot-seam-v1/FINDINGS.txt and comparison-grid.jpg. The corrected recordings
retain the same animation bytes and native mesh weights. Native axial twist is
still approximated. rebuild-status.json identifies the rebuild's completion.
